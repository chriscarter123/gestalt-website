Accessibility shouldn’t be a premium feature.

Sixty-one million Americans live with a disability, and most of the visual art around them — the mural on the corner, the sculpture in the plaza, the collection down the street — has never been described to them. Gestalt exists to close that gap as public infrastructure, not a luxury add-on. Anyone can open Gestalt, point their phone at public art, and hear it.

Capabilities

One accessibility layer for all of a city’s art.

The collection indoors and the murals outside, held to the same standard — recognized, described, and spoken aloud on the phone people already carry.

◎

Proprietary visual model

A visual-recognition model built to work as well inside MoMA as it does on the street — no QR code, no beacon, no staff intervention.

⌖

GPS proximity

Install it and go about your day. As you move through Gestalt-enabled cities, the public art around you comes alive with a curated audio experience.

◈

Automated audio guides

Our accessibility model writes rich, museum-quality descriptions from the image alone, delivered as natural speech with haptic confirmation — so blind and low-vision visitors never need to look at the screen.

▦

Graceful fallbacks

For artworks with unusual forms, institutions and artists can add fallbacks — QR codes, offline mode, and more — so coverage never breaks.

⬡

Multi-venue

Museums, galleries, public spaces, and street art share one unified platform, each with its own location management, floor plans, and accessibility metadata.

♿

Built for every visitor

Audio-first, haptic-confirmed, with zero per-venue hardware cost. Gestalt helps institutions honor their duty to every visitor — and meet the DOJ’s 2024 accessibility rules. Doing right by disabled visitors and meeting the law turn out to be the same work.

Three steps

Point. Discover. Listen.

No accounts or downloads required for the web version. Just open the link and your camera does the rest.

01

Open Gestalt

Open Gestalt on your phone. The camera activates, CLIP loads on-device, and GPS begins tracking your position relative to nearby outdoor artworks.

02

Discover the artwork

Tap “Scan” and the model matches your camera frame against our index in under a second. No QR code, no beacon, no staff. Outdoors, just walk closer and GPS handles it.

03

Listen to the story

Title, artist, year, and a vivid visual description populate instantly. Audio fires with haptic confirmation — the experience works for blind visitors without ever looking at the screen.

A public benefit, by charter

We measure ourselves by access, not just revenue.

The commitments that make Gestalt a public-benefit utility, not a compliance vendor.

✓

Free for the public, forever

The app that lets anyone hear public art is free, with no account required — a commitment in how we operate, not a promotional trial.

✓

Institutions fund the commons

When a museum partners with Gestalt, their support sustains the free public layer and extends description to street art in underserved neighborhoods.

✓

Paid work for disabled describers

Blind and low-vision describers are paid collaborators in building and reviewing our audio — not an afterthought.

✓

No disposable hardware

Gestalt replaces plastic audio-guide handsets and single-use QR placards with software on the phone people already carry.

✓

Open by default

The accessibility metadata we structure is meant to outlive any one venue — a public good, not a private moat.

✓

Human-reviewed, never automated blindly

AI drafts the description; a human describer reviews it. Scale without sacrificing dignity or accuracy.
