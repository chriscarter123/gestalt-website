A public utility for the beauty around us.

Art belongs to everyone. We're making it accessible. Gestalt is an Accessibility Platform that takes an innovative approach to extending culture and fine art to all.

Explore your city. Gestalt will let you know when you're near participating artwork, so you can hear a visually rich description.

Play a description View the Gestalt Lite web app Who it's for

Access is not generosity. It is the removal of a barrier that should not have existed.

We, as a society, have work to do.

The modern world fails so many. With all of the convenience, access, and opportunity, there has been little progress in digital accessibility.

Even less in exploring how technological advances could work in concert with the analog world, as it stands.

Gestalt is a long-term project from Omi Labs (Oro, Omi) focused on developing new products and services in this space.

Gestalt is for

01

Blind and low vision Philadelphians

The city's public art on your own terms, described the moment you stand in front of it. No help required, no one to ask.

02

Neighbors who don't read English

The same wall, explained in a language you actually read, so the city's walls speak to the whole city, not part of it.

03

The city and its institutions

A live, honest picture of what's described and what isn't. The ADA accountability a public collection owes, ready the morning of the meeting.

04

Artists and Mural Arts partners

Every description carries the artist's name and points people back to the work. The wall stays the artist's; Gestalt makes sure more of the city arrives at it.

What a visitor hears.

Persistence, a mural by Jason Andrew Turner: a towering blue wall, a figure in a red dress scattered with white dots. A generic caption would stop at blue wall. Press play and hear what a describer wrote for the person standing in front of it. No front desk, no borrowed device.
