Welcome, DesignPhiladelphia readers

Murals, out loud for everyone in the city.

Gestalt Lite is a free, bilingual audio trail of Philadelphia murals, made for blind and low vision visitors and open to everyone. Walk up to a wall and hear what it holds, in English or Spanish.

Start the mural tour For museums and galleries

How it works

Three steps. No account, nothing to install.

  1. 01

    Open the digital experience

    Go to mural.gestalt.gallery on your phone. It works in the browser you already have.

  2. 02

    Walk to a mural

    Gestalt shows the murals near you, so you can choose where to go next.

  3. 03

    Listen to its description

    Press play and hear the wall described for the person standing in front of it, in English or Spanish.

For museums and galleries

Know where you stand on ADA Title II.

Public museums and galleries run by state and local governments must make their digital content accessible under ADA Title II. Our ADA Readiness Scorecard takes about two minutes and shows where your collection stands, with the gaps to close first and a report you can download and share.
